About

I’m a Computer Engineering graduate from J.C. Bose University of Science and Technology (YMCA), Faridabad. My work is in web application security and automated vulnerability assessment.

From September 2025 to March 2026 I was a testing and cybersecurity intern at STQC (ERTL North), a Government of India laboratory, where I ran penetration tests on live web applications and wrote assessment reports used to prioritise fixes. I build my own security tools in Python, and I am applying to graduate programmes where I can study these problems with a research group.
